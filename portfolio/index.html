Youlify · Product Manager

Ask AI & LLM evaluation

How do you tell whether an AI-generated business answer is good enough to use?

LLM EVALS
Ask AI
Golden setRubricRegression
  1. Define expected answers
  2. Score output
  3. Triage failures
  4. Regression-test fixes

Problem

Conversational analytics gave operations teams a way to ask questions over structured revenue-cycle data. A plausible answer could still use the wrong metric, apply the wrong filter, or draw an unsupported conclusion.

My contribution

I owned the product and evaluation work. I built a scenario-based golden set, wrote scoring rubrics, and grouped recurring failures so engineering and product could distinguish metric, data, prompt, and reasoning issues.

Outcome

The team could turn real failure cases into regression checks. A wrong metric definition could go back to the data or product layer, while a correct metric with an unsupported explanation called for a different fix.

Youlify · Product Manager

Revenue cycle measurement

How do you turn a denial-rate change into an operational decision?

MEASUREMENT
Denial insights
RatesDriversClaims
  1. Track metrics
  2. Identify denial drivers
  3. Inspect claims
  4. Prioritize action

Problem

An aggregate rate tells leaders that performance moved. Teams still need to identify which claims and denial drivers warrant investigation.

My contribution

I built a Command Center for denial rates, financial and operational KPIs, recurring denial patterns, and claim-level drill-downs. I used frequency, claim-dollar exposure, recovery likelihood, and handling cost to prioritize interventions.

Outcome

The team could trace rate changes to recurring denial drivers and individual claims, then prioritize prevention or recovery work. The analysis helped diagnose operational causes.
